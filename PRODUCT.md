# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to inspect, analyze, summarize, and export their own locally stored WeChat data from a desktop workflow.

## Product Purpose

WeFlow is a local-first desktop workspace for WeChat 4.x data. It turns encrypted local chat data into searchable conversations, reports, exports, notifications, and controlled integrations while keeping users aware of what leaves the machine.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a focused desktop utility rather than a marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, oversized promotional typography, ambiguous custom controls, excessive cards, and automation that silently sends private data.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary workflow direct and information-dense.
- Make local versus external data flow explicit.
- Require deliberate opt-in for sensitive or irreversible automation.
- Preserve the existing desktop control vocabulary and theme system.
- Keep generated insights traceable to their source period and delivery result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus and status feedback, readable contrast in light and dark themes, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion compatibility.
